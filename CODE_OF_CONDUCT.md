# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Critique the code, not the person.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing anyone's
private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind — in issues, pull requests, commits,
reviews or any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at the email in `package.json`. Reports are handled privately.
The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block accounts, when this policy is
broken. Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion and is not up for public debate in the issue
tracker.

This is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, kept short deliberately.
